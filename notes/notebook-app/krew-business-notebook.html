    How to learn anything (and make it stick)

    Watching a video once feels like learning, but most of it is gone in a week. This loop is what turns your notes into skills. Run it for every step on the study path.

    1. Watch one chapter, nothing more

      Use the chapter links on each step. One topic per sitting keeps it clear.

    2. Explain it in your own words

      Close the video and write or say it like you're teaching a 12-year-old. The parts you can't explain are the parts you don't know yet. This is called the Feynman technique.

    3. Do one real thing within 24 hours

      Every step has a "Do this for Patrick" task. Doing it is where the lesson actually lands.

    4. Get quizzed

      Ask Claude or Patrick to quiz you on the step. Being tested beats re-reading.

    5. Review on a schedule

      Look at the notes again after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month. Spacing reviews out is the strongest memory trick there is.

    How to get better, week to week

    • One step a week. Nine steps is about two months. Going slower and doing the practice beats racing through.
    • Keep a wins and losses log. Your notes say "lose, lose, lose, win". Write each one down with one sentence on what you learned.
    • Count real things. Conversations with customers, posts made, people using the app, dollars in. Your goal is your first paying customer.
    • Teach Patrick. Tell Patrick what you learned each week. Explaining it is step 2 of the loop, and Patrick remembers it for you.
