## 1. How to start a business with no money
- Start with a feeling.
- Don't start with a business.
- Do what you like. Apply a business mindset to what you love.
- Get good at what you love to do. Get obsessed. **The next step is the idea.**
- Combine / team up with people if needed.
- Link it to your passion.

**Execution**
- Don't make it hard for yourself. Simple execution plan.
- Find where the revenue will come from. Experiment with revenue/incomes.
- Make sure your purpose is strong. Install it into your business.
- Purpose > revenue.

## 2. How to win
- If you're passionate, you're ahead.
- **Gratification:** don't rush to charge customers. Build a relationship with customers. Get customers on your side.
- **Culture:** think "bring value."
- **Luck:** be persistent. Outlast the competition.
- Know your destination. Shoot big.
- Luck is hackable. Learn to hack luck.
- LEARN to take **RISK**. Lean into fear. Take a chance, **DON'T RUSH.**
- Build value. Build a brand, not a business. It needs value for people.

## 3. How to lose
- Learn to bounce back from failure. Learn and accept failure.
- Don't let things own you.
- Let short-term ego go.
- Let them underestimate you. Let them think you're a loser.
- Take your **TIME**.
- Lose, lose, lose, lose, win, lose, win, win…

## 5. How to find purpose
- It's a personal thing. Not easy. People tend not to think about it.
- Think about your purpose: **what problem matters to you?**
- Break down what you do in a day, then find your purpose.
- Purpose is always there [?]. Break free. Truly know yourself.
- Ask: what is that purpose?
- You can team up. Seek people with the same purpose.

## 6. How to find a co-founder
- Accountability.
- Find what you love to do and what you hate to do.
- Same moral code. Find someone who loves what you hate doing.
- Be honest and clear on what you do.
- What are you looking for in a person? Make it clear.
- Define your moral code. **Post it. Look for it.**

## 7. How to sell
- Sell the sizzle, not the steak.
- Get them on your side so they sell to others.
- Sell the outcome.
- **Understand the customer:** do they need you? Get to know them. Do you like them?
- **Deal:** think long term. Learn their business. Be yourself, be honest. Sell what they need.

## 8. How to market *(Krew marked this IMPORTANT)*
- **Experimenting:** connecting, understanding who your customer is.
- **Staircase:** buy the staircase. Do something crazy with the staircase.
- See it (marketing) as a living, evolving thing.
- What's gonna make you stand out? Find it, then evolve it to expand.
- **Systems:** build a system for how you market. Do it well, even if it's one channel. Pick what works for you.
- It's meant to be fun. Enjoy it.

## 9. How to PR your business
- **Targeting:** start local. Find who you're targeting.
- Journalists are busy ("lazy"), so give them what they need. Research the journalist. Find them.
- Make a relationship. Become part of their life.
- **Engage:** think about the image of your project. Learn to *be* the brand.

## 10. How to get an investor
- The more traction, the more likely you'll get an investor.
- Do research.
- **Family and friends:** they know you. Tell the truth, make it clear.
- Early stage, getting **help** is almost more valuable than money.
- Consider future investors.
- **Angel investors:** be very clear what you'll give them. Don't ask for money, **ASK FOR HELP.** Look for the right people and their money.
- **FOMO:** make them think they're missing out. Make them feel needed.
- **VCs:** do they have money? Don't waste time. See who they've invested in before. Try to get connected to a company they've invested in.
- Work with your brand partners. They might pay for your expansion.
- Ask yourself: **do you even need an investor?**

## 11. How to get sponsors
- **Value return:** they get value back.
- **Emotion sell:** understand the brand. What is the brand's value?
- Research the people who run the brands: media buyers, agencies.

## 12. How to build a brand
- Start with your personal brand: your values, what you care about [?]. Take your personal brand seriously.
- Write down the different brand values.
- **Reference:** using someone good, or supporting someone who uses your brand, makes your brand look good.
- Have the ability to cut it out of the business.
- Learn to say **NO.**

## 13. How to hire, grow and build
- **Purpose:** if your business has a purpose, hire people with the same purpose. You'll manage a purpose, not people. Install a purpose.
- Hire people if they care about the purpose.
- Check and do research on the people. Check references.
- Give the team equity.
- **Values & destination:** know your destination. Take risks, try things out. Can't sit still.

## 15. How to go global
- **Research:** can other markets use your product? Find out where your product can be sold.
- **Franchising:** applies to a service business. A bigger business is easier to manage.

## 16. How to get a mentor
- What do you really need? Someone who can answer your questions, or answers questions you don't know to ask.
- Research what matters to that person.
- Define what you mean when asking. Ask a question first.
- **Mentor → Advisor:** connect the people they mentor, referrals. **Give value.**

## 17. How equity works
- Control isn't by equity, it's by the shareholder agreement.
- Don't sell much at the start, or don't sell any.
- **50/50:** don't belittle the other person. Use a 3rd party / board of advisors. Be very clear, work it out, figure out why.
- Shows who's involved. Remember brand image.
- Equity *is* the business. Shares are maybe future equity.

## 18. How to sell your business
- The best way to sell is not wanting to sell it at all.
- Build a business you love; it will sell by accident.
- **Partnership:** partner up with a company that could buy it.
- Don't accept the first offer. Sell emotion.
- **Agents:** research.
- **Merger:** ally with a competitor so they could buy it or merge.
- Allowing the management to buy it.
