# Instagram Playbook: new dog account

*Written Oct 2026. Instagram changes its programs often, so anything marked **verify** should be checked in the app
(Profile → ☰ → Professional dashboard) before you rely on it. The monetization blogs I found disagree with each other.*

## 1. How views work

- A **view** is counted each time your video starts playing (replays count too). It is not a unique person.
  Reach/accounts reached = unique people. Views are the number you see on a Reel.
- **Views don't pay you directly.** Instagram has no "X dollars per 1,000 views" switch you can turn on.
  Money comes from five places, roughly in order of how realistic they are for a new account:
  1. **Brand deals / free products** from pet brands. Often start at ~1–5k followers with good engagement. Most realistic first income.
  2. **Affiliate links** (Amazon Associates, pet-product programs) in your bio/stories.
  3. **Your own products** (merch, printables, treats). This is where your AI-and-apps plan fits.
  4. **Meta payouts**: invite-only bonus programs, Gifts, Subscriptions, and Facebook's Content Monetization
     (Reels cross-posted to Facebook can earn there). Rules, thresholds and countries change. **Verify** in the Professional dashboard.
     Some blogs quote "10,000 followers + 600,000 minutes viewed", which is the Facebook-side threshold, not a guaranteed Instagram one.
  5. Ad revenue share on Reels: not generally open on Instagram. Don't plan around it.
- You must be **18+** for Meta's payout programs, and the account must be **original content** that follows their monetization policies.
- **Realistic expectation:** the first 1,000 followers and the first dollar both take weeks to months. Accounts that earn do it by
  getting followers first and then selling to them, not by waiting for view payouts.

## 2. Settings to do today (15 minutes)

1. **Switch to a Professional account → Creator**, category *Pet* (or "Pet Service"/"Personal blog"). Unlocks Insights and monetization tools.
2. **Public account** (not private). Private accounts can't grow from Reels.
3. **Two-factor authentication** (Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security). Hacked pet accounts are common.
4. **Check Account status** (Settings → Account status). Confirms you're eligible to be recommended to non-followers.
5. **Share Reels to Facebook automatically** (Accounts Center → Sharing across profiles). Free extra reach and it opens Facebook monetization later.
6. **Username**: short, memorable, searchable (e.g. `dogname.daily`). **Bio**: one line saying what people get ("Daily chaos from a golden retriever 🐕") + one link.
7. **Profile photo**: the dog's face, close-up, readable at tiny size.
8. **Remix/Reuse**: leave on. Others reusing your Reels spreads your account.
9. Turn on **Notifications** for comments so you can reply fast in the first hour after posting.

## 3. Growing from zero

**What the algorithm rewards in 2026 (per multiple 2026 guides; treat as directional):**
watch time and replays, shares to DMs ("sends"), saves, and original content. Reposts with TikTok watermarks are down-ranked.

**Do:**
- Post **3–5 Reels a week** for the first 60 days. Consistency beats perfection. Batch-film on one day.
- **Hook in the first 1–2 seconds** (dog doing the weird thing immediately; on-screen text like "He did NOT mean to do this").
- Keep Reels **7–20 seconds** and make them **loopable**. Replays count as views.
- Add **on-screen captions**; most people watch muted. (First feature I'd add to Patrick.)
- Use **original audio or royalty-free music**. Don't use music you don't own if you want to monetize.
- Pick **one niche angle** (a personality: "dog who hates the vacuum", "dog reviews snacks"). A character grows faster than random clips.
- **3–5 relevant hashtags** and a keyword-rich caption ("golden retriever puppy funny"). Hashtag-stuffing doesn't help.
- Use **Trial Reels** (when you share a Reel, choose "Trial") to test a video on non-followers before your followers see it. **Verify** it's available to you.
- **15 minutes a day** commenting genuinely on other dog accounts of similar size, and reply to every comment on yours.
- Ask for sends: "Send this to someone who needs a dog like this."
- Check **Insights → Reels** weekly: which videos got the most watch time and sends? Make more of those.

**Don't:**
- Buy followers or use bots/auto-follow apps. It tanks reach and can get the account banned.
- Repost watermarked TikToks. Delete/ignore the "Instagram says my reach dropped" panic; just post.
- Judge anything in the first 2 weeks. New accounts have no audience data, so early reach is noisy.
