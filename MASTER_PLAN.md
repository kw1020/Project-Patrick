## Revenue Pillar #1: Video Games Explain the Real World

**The idea:** Take real-world topics (money, science, history, business,
psychology, current events) and explain them using the worlds, characters,
and mechanics of the most popular video games. People already love the game;
the game makes the lesson stick.

> Example: "How inflation works, explained with Minecraft villager trading."
> "How supply chains work, explained with Fortnite loot drops."

### It's a franchise, not one channel

Minecraft is the launch game. The format then branches out to every popular
game, each with its own series (and later its own channel if it takes off).

| Phase | Game | Why it fits | Example episode |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (now) | **Minecraft** | Biggest audience, very permissive fan-content rules, easy to animate | Villager trading = inflation & supply/demand |
| 2 | **Terraria** | 2D, cheap to animate, loyal fanbase | Boss progression = how careers/skill trees work |
| 2 | **Roblox** | Huge young audience, creator-friendly | Roblox economy (Robux) = how currencies work |
| 3 | **Fortnite** | Massive reach; UEFN lets you build custom scenes | Storm circle = how resources get scarce |
| 3 | **Call of Duty** | Older teen/adult audience | Loadouts = budgeting & opportunity cost |
| 3 | **Pokémon** | All ages, strong nostalgia | Type matchups = competitive advantage in business |
| 3 | **Mario** | Most recognized game character ever | Power-ups = compound interest / leverage |
| 4 | GTA, Zelda, Among Us, Stardew Valley, Clash Royale, Elden Ring, Sims, etc. | Rotate in whatever is trending | Stardew farm = running a small business |

**Rule for picking the next game:** go where the search volume and trending
videos are. Patrick's research agent checks trends weekly and recommends the
next game/topic combo.

### ⚠️ Know the rules for each game (protects the income)

Getting a channel struck or demonetized kills the revenue, so each game's
fan-content policy matters:

- **Minecraft (Microsoft/Mojang):** Very friendly. Videos and animations using
  Minecraft are allowed and can be monetized. Don't imply official endorsement.
- **Fortnite (Epic):** Fan content policy allows videos; UEFN/Creative content
  has its own rules. Don't sell Epic's characters/art.
- **Call of Duty (Activision):** Gameplay videos generally fine; don't use
  their logos as your brand.
- **Pokémon & Mario (Nintendo / The Pokémon Company):** The strictest.
  Gameplay videos through YouTube's partner program are generally allowed, but
  custom animations of their characters, merch, or ads featuring them are
  risky. **Strategy:** use gameplay footage + commentary, or original
  "inspired-by" characters (a trainer-style kid with made-up creatures, a
  plumber-style hero) instead of their exact characters.
- **Never sell merch with a game company's characters or logos.** Merch must
  be our own original characters/brand.

(Policies change — the research agent re-checks each game's current policy
before we launch that game's series.)

### How it makes money

1. **Ad revenue** — YouTube Partner Program (long-form + Shorts), TikTok
   Creator Rewards, Facebook/Instagram Reels.
2. **Sponsorships** — gaming gear, educational apps, finance apps for teens.
3. **Our own products** — courses, worksheets for teachers/homeschool parents
   ("Minecraft Economics" lesson packs), an app later.
4. **Original mascot + merch** — build our own host character that appears in
   every game's series; merch uses only that character.
5. **Licensing / B2B** — sell the explainer videos to schools and edtech
   platforms.
