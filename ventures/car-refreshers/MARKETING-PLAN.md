# Marketing Plan: Sell Volume on Daily and Signature

**The goal:** most of the money comes from selling a lot of **SMP Daily** ($6) and **Signature** ($38 + Refill Club). **Reserve** ($295) sells few units; its job is to make the whole brand look expensive.

Numbers marked *estimate* are planning figures to check against real results. Check fees in your own TikTok Shop Seller Center: sources differ on whether the referral fee is 6% or 8% in 2026.

---

## 1. The engine

```
See it (TikTok, car meets, car wash counter)
   → Try it cheap: SMP Daily, $6 single in stores / 3 for $15 online
   → QR code on every Daily sleeve: "Upgrade to the metal clip, 15% off"
   → Signature Starter Set, $38
   → Refill Club, $14/month  ← the recurring money
Reserve sits on top: it shows up in content so the $6 card feels like part of something luxury.
```

**The line that ties it together:** *"Midnight Leather starts at $6 and goes up to $295."* Every tier carries the same scent names. This is true and it sells: the cheap card borrows the Reserve's status. Never claim the $6 card *is* the $295 scent; the oil grades differ, and the Reserve is the fine-fragrance version.

## 2. The money per sale (estimates)

| Sale | Price | Product cost | Fees | Shipping | ≈ Profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily single, in a store (wholesale) | You get $3 | $0.40–0.90 | none | bulk box | **~$2.20** |
| Daily 3-pack online | $15 | ~$2.50 incl. pouch | TikTok 6–8% + creator 15% ≈ $3.40 | ~$4 | **~$5** |
| Daily 6-pack online | $25 | ~$5 | ≈ $5.70 | ~$4.50 | **~$9.80** |
| Signature Starter online | $38 | $9–12 | ≈ $8.60 | ~$5 | **~$12–15** |
| Refill Club month | $14 | $1.50–3 | ~$1 (site) | ~$4 | **~$6–7 / month** |

What this means:
- **Never sell a single $6 card online.** Shipping eats it. Online minimum is the 3-pack; push the 6-pack.
- **Singles belong in physical stores**, where the store does the selling.
- **A Signature buyer who stays in the Refill Club 6 months is worth about $50** in profit. You can pay up to about $15 to win one and still do well. One write-up of Drift (a similar car-scent brand) reports about $10 per customer from Facebook ads; that figure is unverified.
- **Make cancelling the Club one click** and email 3 days before every charge. Drift has many public complaints about hard-to-cancel subscriptions; being easy to leave is a selling point.

## 3. Channels, in order of priority

### A. TikTok + TikTok Shop (main channel)
- **Post 1–3 videos a day for the first 60 days.** Same videos to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- **TikTok Shop listings:** Daily 3-pack, Daily 6-pack "Try every scent", Signature Starter, Signature + 3 refills bundle.
- **Affiliate creators:**
  - Open collaboration at **15%** commission. The 2026 US average is about 13%, and car accessories commonly pay 10–18%.
  - Targeted invites at **20%** for the best car and detailing creators.
- **Seeding:** send free Daily 3-packs to 100 car, detailing and Uber/Lyft creators (about $6 each with shipping). Send Signature sets to the 20 best fits.
- **Spark Ads:** once a creator or brand video beats your average, put $20–50/day behind *that* video. Don't run ads on videos that didn't already work.

### B. Stores: Daily singles (cash flow, no shipping)
- **Counter display box of 24 cards**, wholesale **$72 ($3 each)**. The store sells at $6 and keeps 50%, the standard "keystone" markup retailers expect.
- **Targets, in order:** car washes, detail shops, independent gas stations, barbershops, auto parts stores, tire shops, college bookstores.
- **Pitch in one line:** "Premium-looking car scent, $6 impulse buy, you keep half, I restock."
- **Restock route:** visit every 2 weeks; swap slow scents for fast ones.
- **Example (estimate):** 30 stores × 2 boxes a month = 1,440 cards = **$4,320 revenue, about $3,000 profit a month.**

### C. Car meets and Cars & Coffee
- A small table: Daily 3-for-$15, Signature on display, one Reserve under glass.
- "Smell test" jars of each scent.
- A QR code for the Refill Club with a meet-only code.
- Film everything for TikTok: reactions are content.

### D. Detailers and rideshare drivers (repeat buyers)
- **Detail shops:** sell them Daily wholesale to hang in every finished car (their branding on the sleeve later). They're also your Reserve channel.
- **Rideshare drivers:** a "Driver Pack" of 10 Daily cards a month as a subscription, about $40. They need a fresh car every day; ratings depend on it.

### E. Website (Shopify): home of the Refill Club
- Bundles, gift sets, Refill Club, email + SMS list.
- **Refer a friend:** give $5, get $5.
- **First-order offer:** 15% off for email signup.

### F. Later: Amazon (Daily 6-pack, Signature refills) once reviews exist on TikTok Shop and the site.

## 4. What to post: content that sells car scent

**Rule: the scent can't come through the screen, so sell the reaction, the object and the proof.**

**Five content pillars**
1. **Reactions:** passengers, friends, strangers getting in the car.
2. **The object:** the magnetic snap, the gold clip, unboxing, ASMR.
3. **Proof:** the day-1 vs day-30 test in a hot car, the weigh-in on a jewelry scale, honest comparisons.
4. **The lab:** mixing scents in the Design Lab, "help me name this scent," letting followers vote on the next scent.
5. **The story:** building a car scent brand from scratch, day by day; supplier samples arriving; mistakes.

**25 hooks to start with**
1. "Get in my car and tell me what it smells like."
2. "POV: you get in a car that smells like it costs six figures."
3. "$6 vs $295 car scent. Can you tell the difference?"
4. "Rating my friends' cars by smell."
5. "The sound this makes is the whole reason I bought it." (magnetic snap)
6. "I left this in a 160-degree car for 30 days. Here's what's left."
7. "Things in my car that make people ask what I drive."
8. "My Uber rating went up after I changed one thing."
9. "Detailers: the last step nobody does."
10. "Which scent are you, based on your car?"
11. "I'm building a car scent brand. Day 1."
12. "The samples from 4 factories just came. Only one is good."
13. "Help me name this scent." (lab screen recording)
14. "You voted, I made it." (follower-chosen scent)
15. "Why your car freshener dies in a week." (heat, gel, liquid)
16. "Little Trees vs this." (side-by-side; compare, don't trash)
17. "What $295 gets you in a car scent." (Reserve close-ups, makes Daily feel premium)
18. "Smell of a brand-new car, but it lasts."
19. "Girlfriend rates my car's smell."
20. "Car meet smell test." (strangers, blind)
21. "The cheapest upgrade for your car's interior."
22. "Tesla owners: your vents don't hold clips. Here's the fix."
23. "3 scents for 3 moods: date night, gym, road trip."
24. "Packing 100 orders in my garage."
25. "The car freshener that matches your car's interior."

**Rules for every video:** hook in the first second, the product on screen by second 3, the price shown, the TikTok Shop link pinned, no designer-brand or carmaker names in captions or on screen.

## 5. Offers and launches

| Moment | Offer |
|---|---|
| Launch week | Daily 3-pack $12 (normally $15), Signature $32 launch price (as on the site) |
| Every month | "Scent of the month": a limited scent, Daily only, gone when gone |
| Gifting | Gift box (Signature + 2 refills + 3 Daily) for Christmas, Valentine's, Father's Day, graduation |
| New drivers | "First car" bundle for 16th birthdays and new licenses |
| Refill Club | First month $9; free Daily 3-pack every 3rd month to keep people in |
| Referral | Give $5, get $5 |

## 6. First 60 days

| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Set up TikTok, Instagram and the TikTok Shop account. Post 1/day about building the brand (samples, lab, factory messages). Make a list of 100 creators and 50 local stores. |
| 3–4 | Samples in hand: post the object, reactions, hot-car test start. Send creator seeding. Walk into the first 10 stores with a display box. |
| 5–6 | Launch: shop live, launch prices, 2–3 posts/day. First car meet. Turn on Spark Ads behind the best video. |
| 7–8 | Double down on the top 3 video formats. Restock stores; add 10 more. First Refill Club renewals: watch how many stay. |

**Starting marketing budget (estimate): about $1,500–2,000.**
- creator seeding: about $600 Daily + $340 Signature
- Spark Ads: about $600 for the first month
- printed display boxes for 30 stores: about $90
- car meet table: $0–150 per event

## 7. Numbers to watch weekly

- **Views → shop clicks → orders** per video. Kill formats that don't convert.
- **Cost per new Signature customer.** Keep it under about $15.
- **Refill Club:** how many stay after months 1, 2 and 3.
- **Stores:** cards sold per box per 2 weeks, and reorders.
- **Best scents per channel:** stores and TikTok may want different ones.
