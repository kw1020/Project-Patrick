# Supplier Outreach Drafts

Six messages, one per supplier type. Each asks for samples and a quote. Copy, fill in the [brackets], and send to 3–4 suppliers of each type so you can compare.

**Where to send them**
| # | Supplier | Where to find them | Search terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signature clip + ceramic discs | Alibaba, Global Sources (message the supplier) | "zinc alloy car vent diffuser custom logo", "magnetic car aroma diffuser", "porous ceramic diffuser disc" |
| 2 | Daily paper cards | Alibaba, Global Sources | "custom scented paper car air freshener", "car air freshener foil pouch custom print" |
| 3 | Reserve brass bodies | CNC services (Xometry, PCBWay, JLCCNC) or a local machine shop | Upload the spec as an RFQ ("request for quote") |
| 4 | Fragrance oils (Daily + Signature) | US wholesale fragrance oil companies | "wholesale fragrance oil IFRA certificate", "car diffuser fragrance oil" |
| 5 | Perfumer (Reserve) | Independent perfumers: Instagram, Basenotes forums, perfumery school alumni | "independent perfumer custom fragrance commission" |
| 6 | Packaging | Alibaba, Packlane, local box makers | "rigid magnetic gift box custom foil", "leather case custom small" |

**Rules for every message**
- Never name a designer brand or carmaker. Not in the brief, not in examples.
- Ask for **samples first**. Don't pay for production until the 30-day test is done.
- Get every price **in writing**, including setup fees, molds, shipping and tax.
- Don't share your full sales plan or volumes beyond what's in the draft.

## 5. Perfumer (Reserve fine fragrance)

**Subject:** Commission inquiry: fine fragrance for a luxury car scent collection

Hello [name],

I came across your work and would love to talk about a commission. I'm launching Sutherland Motor Parfum, a car fragrance brand whose top line, Reserve, is a solid brass diffuser made for owners of high-end cars. It sells for $295. I'm looking for a perfumer to create its first fragrances.

**The first scent: "Paddock"**
Warm leather in the pit lane. It opens with saffron, cardamom and a little pink pepper. The heart is black leather. It dries down to smoked wood and amber. In the car it should read as quietly expensive: soft and close, never loud.

My working formula by proportion: black leather 25%, saffron 19%, smoked wood 19%, amber 19%, cardamom 12%, pink pepper 6%. Treat this as a direction, not a recipe.

**Second scent to discuss: "Agar"**

**Technical constraints**
- The carrier is a porous black stone insert (no liquid in the car), and it should last 6–8 weeks

- I'd like natural materials (oud, saffron, rose, iris) where IFRA allows, since that's what justifies the price
- IFRA Category 12 certificate, SDS and allergen list for the final formula

**Questions**
1. Do you take commissions like this, and what's your fee structure (development fee, per-kg price, ownership of the formula)?
2. Typical timeline from brief to final mod?
3. Minimum batch size once the formula is approved?

First run would be 50–100 Reserve sets, growing from there. Happy to send the brass prototype and the stone insert so you can test on the real carrier.
