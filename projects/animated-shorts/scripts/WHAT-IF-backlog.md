# "What if" backlog (Krew's style: put YOU in it)

Pick from here for the next batches; strike through when scripted. Each must have real science behind it.

## You + a power
- What if you could teleport? (what happens to the air where you land)
- What if you were invisible? (you'd be blind: light has to stop in your eyes to see)
- What if you could fly like a bird? (chest muscles the size of ...)
- What if you could stop time? (no air moves: you couldn't breathe or push through it)
- What if you had a perfect memory? (real people with HSAM: they can't forget bad days)
- What if you could breathe underwater?
- What if you were as strong as an ant? (square-cube law, links to batch 02 #05)

## You + your body
- What if you never ate again?
- What if you drank only coffee?
- What if you stayed in bed for a year? (NASA bed-rest studies)
- What if you lost your sense of fear? (patient S.M., amygdala damage)
- What if you never cut your hair or nails?
- What if you went to space without a suit? (about 15 seconds of consciousness)

## You + the world
- What if you were the only person who could see the future? 
- What if everyone on Earth jumped at the same time? (nothing happens: real numbers)
- What if you fell into the ocean's deepest point?
- What if you lived on Mars for a year?
- What if the internet went down forever?
- What if all the ice melted? (map of your coast)
- What if you were born 10,000 years ago?

## The world ends (one per month at most, keep it light)
- What if the Earth stopped spinning?
- What if a black hole came to the solar system?
- What if all the bees disappeared?
- What if the Sun became a black hole? (orbit doesn't change)
